# Email PO to Invoice Demo

This public demonstration will show how Iconwizard AI can coordinate a business process from an incoming purchase-order email through approval, work execution and invoice preparation.

## Demonstration stages

1. Receive a simulated customer email and purchase order.
2. Extract supplier, order, item and delivery information.
3. Flag missing or uncertain fields for review.
4. Request approval from an authorised user.
5. Create and assign a work order.
6. Record completion confirmation.
7. Prepare an invoice draft.
8. Request accounts approval.
9. Simulate sending the approved invoice.

## Safety boundaries

- The demonstration uses fictional data only.
- Email sends and financial postings must be simulated unless explicitly configured in a secure private environment.
- AI extraction results remain subject to validation and human approval.
